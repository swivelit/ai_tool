# Company Knowledge Base - Voice Agent AI Platform

## About Us
Voice Agent AI is a next-generation AI-powered onboarding platform. We help businesses create seamless, conversational onboarding experiences using natural language processing and voice technology. Our platform speaks Tanglish (Tamil + English) natively for South Indian users.

A: Yes! Our platform is fully responsive and works on any mobile browser. No app download needed.

Q: How accurate is the voice recognition?
A: Our speech-to-text system is optimized for Tamil and Tanglish with over 95% accuracy for clear audio.

Q: Can I customize the questions?
