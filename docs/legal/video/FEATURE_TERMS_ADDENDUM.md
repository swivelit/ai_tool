The website template-video feature is restricted to authenticated adults who
confirm that every depicted source person is an adult, that the source photos
may be uploaded and used, and that the requester has clear specific permission
from the depicted people or authority to act for them. The requester must
also have permission to modify and distribute the selected video and its
audio.

The output is synthetic/AI-edited media. Swico adds a prominent visible
disclosure and a non-sensitive provenance identifier where technically
feasible. The disclosure is not removable through Swico and the output must
not be represented as authentic evidence or an unedited recording.

Minors, non-consensual intimate imagery, sexual exploitation, deceptive
impersonation, fraud, false records, harassment and unlawful harm are
prohibited. A source photo, template file or face consent does not itself
prove copyright, publicity, music, movie or distribution permission.
