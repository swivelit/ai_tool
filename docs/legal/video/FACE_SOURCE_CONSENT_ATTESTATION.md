# Source-face consent checklist

The website records these confirmations for every request under the current
consent version:

- requester is at least 18 and authorised to submit the request;
- every depicted source face is an adult;
- each depicted person gave permission for this specific edit, or the
  requester has clear specific authority to act for them;
- requester has rights to upload and use each source photo;
- requester has permission to modify and distribute the selected video and
  audio;
- output is synthetic/AI-edited, visibly disclosed and not authentic evidence;
- prohibited-use restrictions are accepted;
- temporary retention and expiry are understood; and
- disclosure/provenance must not be removed or obscured.

These checkboxes are user attestations, not a substitute for documentary
rights evidence, identity verification, counsel advice or a court order.
Swico must keep the version and acceptance time with the job and reject stale
or incomplete consent before admission.
