# Acceptable use and abuse handling

Do not use template-video for minors, non-consensual intimate imagery,
sexual exploitation, deceptive impersonation, fraud, false records,
misleading verification, harassment, threats or unlawful harm. Do not remove
the synthetic-media disclosure or present the result as an authentic original.

Reports may be sent to `privacy@swiveltechnologies.in` for privacy, source
consent, minor, intimate-image, impersonation and takedown concerns;
`support@swiveltechnologies.in` for technical abuse; and
`billing@swiveltechnologies.in` for payment issues. Include the opaque result
identifier or authenticated account information where available, but do not
email unnecessary source photos or identity documents.
