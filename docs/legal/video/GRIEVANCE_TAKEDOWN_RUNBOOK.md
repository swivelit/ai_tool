# Internal grievance and takedown runbook

This is an operating procedure, not a public promise or counsel opinion. The
operator must map the final public contact and response commitments to the
applicable law before rollout.

## Intake and triage

1. Record the time, channel, requester contact, result provenance identifier,
   account/job reference and the narrowest description needed to investigate.
2. Do not request or redistribute source images unnecessarily. Restrict access
   to the smallest support/legal group and preserve an audit trail.
3. Treat reports involving a minor, non-consensual intimate imagery, credible
   impersonation, immediate safety risk or unlawful disclosure as urgent.
4. Target internal acknowledgement within four business hours and urgent
   triage within one hour when staff coverage permits. These are internal
   operating targets, not a replacement for any statutory deadline.

## Containment and decision

1. Restrict authenticated access to the result while the report is verified;
   do not silently alter the source or remove the provenance record.
2. Preserve the job, consent version, output hash, relevant event records and
   complaint evidence under the existing retention and access controls.
3. Verify account ownership and request only evidence reasonably needed to
   establish consent, rights, identity or legal authority.
4. Escalate urgent or legally complex reports to the accountable operator and
   qualified adviser. Do not invent a Grievance Officer or claim counsel
   review.
5. Restore, retain, disable or remove access only under documented policy,
   verified consent/rights, a valid legal request, safety necessity or an
   accountable owner decision. Record the reason and time.

## Follow-up

Provide a safe status update when investigation takes time, apply the response
period required by applicable law, and record refund/credit handling
separately. If a report exposes a product-control failure, keep the feature
paused until the release gates are rechecked.
