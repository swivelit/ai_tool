# Internal template-rights evidence checklist

This blank checklist is for the accountable operator to complete with genuine
documents. It is not evidence and must not be copied into production as if it
were completed.

For each imported template, retain privately:

- real reviewer identity or accountable role;
- review date;
- permission to modify the video;
- permission to distribute the resulting video;
- permission for the audio/music in the resulting video;
- source document filenames and SHA-256 values; and
- any separate model/performer/face rights evidence required by the audit.

Face consent does not replace movie, performer, copyright, audio or music
rights. The local rights CLI copies evidence into private storage, hashes the
copied bytes and invalidates approval/calibration when evidence changes.
